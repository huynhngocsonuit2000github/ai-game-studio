# Acceptance criteria and reviews

| Gate | Technical exit criteria | Producer / publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 | T01; coherent GDD, art and technical feasibility; exact pack, referenced evidence and dates | Actual scoped design + repository-media authorization, supplied when Producer pastes full prompt. Pages pending. |
| G1 | T02–T10/T12 for slice subset. Start, move/dash, learn, one rite, boss, loss and success work. Resume or retry resets state. | No standalone review inside the explicitly named batch. |
| G2 | Present a 04:00–05:30 polished slice with actual title/combat/draft/boss/result, playable victory/defeat, Comet recipe, final art, audio and settings. T11–T17 and T20/T21 for scope. Provide screenshot evidence, stage checkpoint and scoped PR. | Human playtest and acceptance before S04. Record technical candidate separately from acceptance. |
| G3 | Data-driven normal game uses every approved feature, 16 skills, 3 ward positions, 5 normals, 2 elites, full boss, cosmetics and stats. T04–T10/T12/T17/T18. No open P0/P1 or unapproved placeholder. | Record decisions for content complete and further scope. |
| G4 | T19–T22 as applicable, clean full assembly, actual public routes/covers, settings/storage, budgets, rights/credits, rollback and clear evidence status. A release candidate must pass pre-merge checks before Producer approval; post-merge live smoke is a separate result. | Producer approves release and applicable Pages visibility explicitly. Merge is manual. Human playtest required for final acceptance. |

PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN/BLOCKED applies to technical criteria. If hardware and real deployment are absent, state which tests remain. Record resolved deviations and approvals with exact builds. If blocked, complete independent authorized work, retain evidence, open a draft when supported, and stop after at most three repeated repair attempts without new evidence.
