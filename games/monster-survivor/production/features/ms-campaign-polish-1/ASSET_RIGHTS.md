# Armory art provenance

Four original game-local lantern portraits were generated for this brief with OpenAI ImageGen from a text-only prompt. The source is the 1254×1254 transparent sheet exec-d74ee8ea-5eb9-4fc7-b7ba-504079abaded.png. No external source image, logo or font was used. The pack includes only transparent 384×384 WebP exports cropped from the original, resized with preserved aspect ratio and centered with padding. Use them for inventory and Forge showcase and optionally for a downscaled pickup. No source sheet or temporary file should ship to Pages. Usage and redistribution are subject to the applicable OpenAI Output Terms and the Producer's external asset grant MSCP-AP01. That grant covers the four exact portraits in the public repo and existing Pages preview subject to manual merge. A pack manifest declares the authorization expected at handoff and is not evidence of a decision by itself.
