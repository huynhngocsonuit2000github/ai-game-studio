At 1280×720, target board width 930–1010 px, notebook width 170–210 px and clear margin. A 44 px or larger touch target for interactive tiles/buttons is a hard target at the existing 844×390 landscape acceptance viewport. Prefer a compact two-column notebook 118–145 px wide, board geometry aligned with visual rows, and fit vertically without exposing major scroll during the fight. Reserve range preview and selected tile for input; no intrusive overlays over the breach edge. At smaller landscape sizes where 44 px cannot all fit, retain legible cards and touch gestures using shared hit geometry. Preserve working portrait rotate hint and game state. Keyboard focus must remain distinct from plant selection. Use Vietnamese-capable system type and 4.5:1 text contrast on actual backgrounds. Test all prices with the widest label and large OS text scaling.
