# GH-RETRO-01 v2 · Sổ tay Nhà Kính

A dedicated Vietnamese help page available **before entering combat**, from the title and campaign map. During combat, the Pause menu opens the same guide as a paused view. Back returns to the exact originating screen and restores focus. From combat it returns to Pause; the player explicitly resumes. Open normal Play in one click as before. Never force reading, play guide sounds over the music, reset the match, or reveal boss details without opting in.

## Page layout and data

- In the original warm-paper garden notebook style, show heading `Sổ tay Nhà Kính`, a visible `Quay lại` button, and three switchable sections `Cây`, `Quái`, `Kỹ năng`. Use keyboard-accessible tabs or links with selected semantics and browser navigation. On a game-hosting subpath use the current client routing convention or a relative query/hash. Deep entry lands safely, close returns to title when there is no in-game origin, and the portal return link remains relative. Include a small document title and visible focus. Page normal scroll. At phone widths, choose stacked list/detail and avoid forced landscape for reading.
- Browse the six plants and six enemies with recognizable portraits and roles, filter by role or type, and search by name (Vietnamese typed with or without accents, including đ/d). For a first-use entry, immediately show its role, cost/availability where relevant, and a `Dùng khi` or `Đối phó` line. The detail area gives strengths, limits, recommended placement, counters, combinations or phase tells. Keep answers visible and concise. Let a short preview selector choose e.g. `Đứng yên`, `Ra đòn`, `Nhận đòn`, `Rút lui`. Add `Xem chuyển động` to start a muted animation only on demand and `Dừng` to freeze it. Under reduced motion, use named static key poses. Focus remains on the clicked button, no scroll jump.
- Use supplied `FIELD_GUIDE_VI.json` entries for prose. Derive numeric badges at build/runtime from canonical final tuning. Never ship unlinked duplicate costs, cooldowns, caps or weakened mechanic descriptions after balancing. Validate guide IDs, defined tab order, cross-links, media keys and level/role mapping at build/test. Hide detailed Keeper text or image behind `Hiện chi tiết boss`, including when searched. Mark locked cards by first stage or in-stage reveal; the manual remains readable. See canonical game docs for actual star rules and data.
- Link accessible entry points: on title and map, and inline `Xem trong Sổ tay` in the deck-selection info and available card detail/readout. These are menus/buttons outside simulation or deliberate pause, not accidental inputs on the battlefield. By intent, browsing increases player understanding without adding a step between title and entering the board.

## Runtime animation contract

Use the supplied original raster character art and the included WebP motion sheets under `assets/runtime/art/retro/motion/`. Each contains fixed-size transparent frames (typically six columns), and `atlas.json` declares each clip, tick timing, loop behavior, anchor, and suggested reduced-motion pose. The atlas frames are authored cutout gestures derived from the approved characters. The actual renderer layers projectile/impact/glow, foliage, water and cue effects on top, with event alignment and game rules. An idle bob is only the idle pose; use every corresponding actual state, including player-visible defeat.

- Resolve animation state from simulation tick and explicit state transitions/events. Ordinary movement actually alternates pose as unit travels, enemy slowed/rooted/stunned remains held for true durations, attack pose has recognizable anticipation/action/recovery. If the timing engine applies damage immediately, windup is secondary anticipation only when an attack is actually scheduled; do not shift combat balance to sync art. Separate guide preview clock from simulation. Canvas/DOM effects do not mutate tick, hitboxes or score.
- Keep base anchors and hit positions aligned. A dying unit leaves gameplay immediately and emits one bounded visual record, allowing the animation to finish without delaying win, hiding results, or blocking lanes. Art-only scorch and shake must never obscure approachable lanes or make prompts flash. State priority for a representative overlap is death > boss phase/telegraph > attack/ability > damage reaction > restrained > movement/idle; higher cues may use a layer for simultaneous information. Avoid resetting frame 0 each redraw. Paint from actual ticks. Pose bookmarks should not advance during pause or tab-hidden. Use one shared atlas cache including guide previews; lazy load on stage/guide entry; handle decode failures and show safe static images. Never play every portrait animation at once. Apply bounds to particles and event ghosts.
- Keep the assets in `art/retro/motion` at build time under the Pages project subpath. Include the single compiled guide JSON and only used media. The assembled release excludes sources, design prose, concept art and any authoring tools. Preserve Sân Mưa/Hầm Bơm/Đài Chớp save rules, prior scores, release and scope requirements from the parent spec.
