## What the research changes

| Evidence | Decision for this game |

| EA describes the 2009 original as lane-based tower defense with a humorous cast. Its modern remaster surfaces input shortcuts and a bulk collection option. | Draw from the genre's humor, silhouettes and plant/defend/collect rhythm. Offer keyboard and touch controls, plus low-friction guaranteed automatic credit for unclaimed drops. |

## Feel and first minute

- Title has `Chơi`, `Chọn màn` and Settings. Chơi starts the first uncompleted unlocked stage, or opens the map after all three. A returning player can use the map to replay any earned stage. No extra briefing on normal Play or Next. Put optional details in the map and pause help.
- 6 s prep. First foe enters at combat t=4 on L1 with a little lane flag and audible cue. Plan for first ordinary placement in ≤10 s, first enemy visible ≤12 s from start and first attack in ≤18 s when Fern is planted on the hinted lane within the first 10 s. Tutorial pauses no timers and never charges stock.
- Three starter cards immediately: Fern, Pitcher, Bramble. Show one action at a time for the first run. Light the threatened row and hover ghost for Fern if idle, then cue a nearby loose Ánh Hạt once it appears. After the actual action, remove its cue promptly. Hint on selection/placement/credit success, never just elapsed time. Use new text only if needed and avoid repeated screen-reader announcements. Orchid becomes available at wave 3 warning on L1, with a short reveal; the map previews its role.
- Teach two meaningful actions before the opening pressure: place a plant and collect a drop. Successful spends, valid snaps, hits, shield chips, chain and rooting all have distinct visual and sonic responses. Threat labels use an icon plus one short Vietnamese string. Give a short batch settle beat after a major salvo while the next threat remains visible.

## Data and mechanics

Implement from TUNING_SEED.json. Its numbers and authored spawn times are v0 targets, to be iterated against normal-speed clears and logged in canonical design/TUNING.json. Timed groups may overlap between waves; wave progress advances on the next authored wave start. For pump faults, atSeconds names the closure tick, with warningSeconds counted backwards, preserving current runtime semantics. All times are combat-relative after prep. The full Sổ tay is available before combat from title and map, and from the pause menu. Keep optional browse separate from Play. The guide and its animated previews cannot advance simulation or consume game stock. Validate IDs, shape, one boss, schedules/order/non-overlap of fault windows and pass data through the current build. Add typed fields only where behavior needs them.

**Economy.** Pitcher releases 18 Ánh Hạt every 10 s after placement or combat start, whichever is later. Max 3 live Pitchers globally; full cap disables attempted placement without consuming funds/cooldown. Scheduled ambient drop is every 10 s of combat, first at combat t=3. The six opening drops are 28 each, then 16 each; use a small deterministic list of tile centers rotating among lanes. Ordinary kills produce no income. Porters, menders and raincoats can release 6 at their within-board clamp location, with at most 2 simultaneous elite reward drops; skip extra elite drops, since they are bonus. Inventory remains integer. Every drop has identity, position, value, creation tick and a 3 s claim window. Click/tap now for immediate credit, or it automatically credits the full value at expiry. No mandatory clicking. An accessible `Thu Ánh Hạt` button and S key collect all visible drops, respecting the dialog/focus controls. Collection does not clear selected card, plant through a token, change a valve or debit stock. Process active drop commands before expiry; repeated taps, auto/manual race and expiry+restart cannot duplicate value. At most 8 on-field drops, with overflow from ambient/Pitcher credited immediately. Drops, cooldowns and awards advance only during unpaused simulation. Use visual +number to the res meter, accessible feedback throttled, and the new collecting sound.

**Water.** L1: no valves, no wet-economy rules. Orchid automatically chains to one additional eligible target, and its in-game description varies by stage. From L2, route water as usual to boost Orchid to three additional targets and enable wet Moss regeneration. Pitcher income is independent of water in all stages. Shorten base valve cooldown to 3 s. Raincoat immunity and original jumps skip logic still apply. L2 introduces single-lane faults with a lane badge, warning and countdown; add phase lock art and retain two separable closure sources during L3. Keeper can still be defeated with physical plants when the dangerous lane is locked.

**Cards and attackers.** Planting uses original stable unit IDs and the seed table. Put card price, disabled reason, role, cooldown sweep and actual ready state on each rendered card. Use valid-tile ghost. Programmatic movement uses seeded or deterministic timing, not an animation hit test. Draw wind-up with feedback before resolving attacks, respecting simulation timing. Telegraph Courier fast entry with scarf flutter and small chevrons. Give Porter a shield chip cue and Fern lob as the dependable armored counter. Bramble controls rushes, Moss holds a threatened lane, Pod trades short-lived preparation for burst. L1 unlocks Orchid mid-stage, L2 teaches Moss/water and L3 offers Pod from prep. Per-card placement locks run only after successful placement. The remove action gives existing 25% floor refund, with a single explicit mode and cancel. Draw some air between adjacent units at footprint level; feedback read order is enemy role, impending hit, damage attribution.

**Comeback.** One original Chuông Gốm charge for the entire attempt. The first ordinary enemy crossing the core consumes it, removes only that enemy, no income, and stuns other ordinary enemies in that lane for 1.2 s; stuns halt movement and bites. If two cross in one tick, use sorted stable entity order and consume only once. Keeper bypasses the bell and retains lethal core damage. Show bell separately from Hạt Tim, ring + wave toward source lane, give a readable warning when spent. Retry restores bell and transients.

**Pacing and payoff.** Use quiet to contrast anticipation, never a long empty wait. From first enemy until final scheduled spawn, no authored spawn gap over 18 s and no run can have >10 s with neither a live threat nor an approaching wave cue across the representative approved routes. Prioritize late mixed wave lanes with armored pressure and fast attackers rather than flat HP inflation. Seed aims: Sân Mưa ~4:20–5:40 active, Hầm Bơm ~4:40–6:05, Đài Chớp ~5:10–7:00. Balance using two L1 openers (Pitcher + Fern and double Fern), a slow-defense failure, and one successful route on L2/L3. A small stable economy can win, while attempting a fourth collector cannot trivialize pressure. Check that waves 5–6 force new action or a credible breach risk, and report an observed test route where ignoring them fails or spends the bell. No new currencies, forced grinding or shop systems. Stars: clear (one), ≥2 Hạt Tim (one), at least 3 targets hit by one Orchid arc from L2 or 2 targets hit on L1 (one). Earlier star records retain their provenance separately.
