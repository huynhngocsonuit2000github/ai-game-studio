- The Producer message on 27 November 2026 approved the concept and public development preview for this game and its original assets. Work covers S00–S02, Mine 1 and the working shop. G2 playtest and G4 release acceptance are pending. Work stops before building Mines 2–5.
- MVNL-001 SHA-256 is `0a94996a3bcb9d644dd510b6a8bb4c276525b5310d3b07affb4f7c67013ec542`. The Producer-supplied attachment has six payload files. Every declared path, length and hash matched. There were zero replacements, duplicates, symlinks or unlisted payload files. The pack baseline matches inspected GitHub main `ffc924d799d593a9918d079e9cb11daf62f94451` and the game path and ID were free.
- The Studio ZIP importer rejected the original legacy manifest because its scope and publication fields differ from the external approval. Its uppercase pack ID, string scope, art destinations and omitted per-file rights fields also differ from the current schema. The archive was preserved as received. The six read-only, inspected payload files were used through the Producer's direct request and rights approval. The four verified PNGs went to `public/art` and the production records were retained with a trailing-space cleanup in the design. [Imported provenance](ASSETS.json) records the earlier pending status at packaging time. This decision records the subsequent clearance.
- The shop exit starts a labeled Mine 1 practice shift to verify carryover. It preserves wallet and inventory and resets the mine quota and clock. Buying a potion or magnet arms it, and the shop lets players disarm or re-arm stock before the next shift.
- The trusted base grants no registry-registration exception. The single game-scoped PR carries catalog metadata for Studio preflight. Canonical public catalog registration requires separate Studio review.
