# Mỏ Vàng Náo Loạn
A single-player, one-button browser arcade game. An overconfident miner works a rattling hand winch over a cutaway cavern. Each catch should be identifiable by silhouette before a player reads its value. Use warm amber lanterns, plum stone outlines and pale mint steel. Give the winch distinct empty, heavy and jackpot reactions. Show current shortfall beside the clock. All player-facing text is Vietnamese.

## Rules
Start with an empty wallet. On delivery, credit cash to both current mine earnings and wallet. Quota uses mine earnings alone. Clock runs until zero even after meeting quota; extra catches fund supplies. At zero, credit only deliveries already at the surface, then resolve the result once. Fail/retry restores the mine-entry snapshot (wallet, items, layout and chest seed); shop purchases and catches cannot duplicate. Save between mines and after purchases. Victory follows mine five. Pause when hidden and resume on input. Support replay from mine one.

The hook sweeps a 136-degree arc. Press Space or tap the playfield to launch along the current angle; first contact latches and reels automatically. Empty casts return at the boundary. Ignore held Space and simultaneous pointer/key repeats. During a loaded return, a fresh press spends one owned pair of scissors and drops the catch for zero credit. Show the cut action only while available. Potion and magnet are armed in the shop and activate on the next mine's first cast, at most one of each per mine. Inventory from chests is available for the next shop, except scissors which work immediately. A chest is opened only on delivery.

## Economy and route tuning
Small nugget 100, medium 220, boulder-sized nugget 400, rock 15. Deep chest rolls cash 350 (50%), one uniformly selected supply (30%), or a rock worth 15 (20%). Never require a favorable chest to clear a mine. Potion 140 gives 1.8x loaded reel speed for 12 s; scissors 60 buys one cut; magnet 90 slows aim sweep to 55% for 8 s, retaining full reach. Timed effects pause with the game. Shop shows prices, wallet, inventory, duration and a skip button. Touch targets at least 44 CSS px. Space-only shop: scan cards and exit in a stable order with an obvious focus ring every 1.2 s; Space selects, then releases before rearming. Also support native focus navigation and direct taps.

## Five authored mines
|Mine|Time / quota|Composition and teaching moment|
|---|---|---|
|1. Bãi Vàng Đầu Ca|75 s / 600|Opening cast has a clear small nugget. Place three close pairs, two medium nuggets, one large and a deep chest between two rocks. Show release timing, first payout and cut hint.|
|2. Hầm Đá Kẹt Dây|65 s / 850|Three heavy rocks screen a central 400. Side corridors each hold three small nuggets and one medium. Bottom chest is visible through a narrow opening. A missed angle makes scissors valuable.|
|3. Mỏ Băng Trơn|75 s / 1,000|Hook slides in sweep direction for 0.14 s after release before flight; show spark skid and short aim trail. Two broad shallow lanes teach compensation before a narrow deep lane. Two large, three medium, four small.|
|4. Túi Nước Ngầm|80 s / 1,200|Submerged treasure drifts horizontally in bounded corridors, reverses smoothly and stops drifting on latch. Bubbles communicate direction. Two large, four medium, six small, two rocks and chest.|
|5. Sào Huyệt Chuột|90 s / 1,400|One rat telegraphs with dust for 1.2 s then drags a nugget toward its den. Catching the nugget cancels the theft. Cap permanent theft at two small nuggets. Three large, four medium, six small and one chest. Keep an accessible 1,600 in guaranteed loot.|

## Prototype fixture and production checks
At 8.0 seconds remaining and 200 short, start with a centered idle hook, two unobstructed 100 nuggets slightly either side of center and a chest deeper between them. Tune both safe casts including aiming, outward travel, reel and reset to 6–7.5 seconds combined. Deep chest cycle 5–7 seconds, including aiming. Fix seed for cash, item and rock runs. On a fresh run the choice must be readable without opening a menu. Instrument cast/reel times and bank events at 30 and 60 fps; use the same simulation and input path as live gameplay. Record at least one actual success on each viable route and one failed gamble. Seeded test fixture supplements a complete normal run. Playtest whether a first-time player can name both choices and why they chose one.

Prototype mine 1 and a between-mine shop, then polish it into a vertical slice with the included sprites, original drawn miner, moving winch, tension on the loaded line, short pickup chime, timed item indicators and reduced-motion/mute controls. Stop at G2 for Producer playtest. After G2 build mines 2–5, test each gimmick and the normal five-mine progression, save/reload, retry and victory. G4 requires a human playtest on the published candidate. Target 60 fps desktop, 30 fps on a representative midrange phone and <3 MB initial compressed transfer. Measure after integration. No backend or paid dependencies.
