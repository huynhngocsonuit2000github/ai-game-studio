# Game design

[MVNL-001 gameplay design](../production/DESIGN.md) defines the controls, economy, shop and five authored mines. The current candidate contains Bãi Vàng Đầu Ca and a labeled practice repeat that exercises carryover and supplies.

During a normal shift, the first centered cast teaches the cash feedback. Small gold offers fast and dependable progress, heavier gold requires more time on the line, and rocks can consume enough clock to make scissors valuable. The clock stays active after quota is met so excess gold can pay for supplies.
