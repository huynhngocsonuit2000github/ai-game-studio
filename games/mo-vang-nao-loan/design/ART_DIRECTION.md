# Art direction

The mine is a cutaway beneath timber rails, with a hand winch, an amber lantern, plum strata and mint steel. Use bright warm values for gold, cold matte values for rock and a separate chest silhouette. Value labels reinforce those silhouettes.

The miner leans on a loaded return, the spool follows the rope, and an empty line flexes. Color, motion, sound and on-screen payout feedback distinguish a miss, a heavy catch and a jackpot. Reduced motion removes decorative movement while retaining aim and rope travel. [Asset sources and clearance](../production/ASSET_MANIFEST.csv)
